About

Systems people, in the same industries, for a long time.

Commerceworks has been building and running business systems from the UK and the United States since 2008 — Salesforce, Microsoft, and the connective tissue between them.

What we are

A consultancy that stays. Most of our work is not a project with an end date — it is running the systems a business depends on, week after week, and extending them as the business changes. Our longest engagement has outlasted several of the systems it connects.

That shapes how we work. We are not optimising for a handover document. We are optimising for the thing still being reliable in five years, which is a different job and usually a quieter one.

Where we work

Downstream energy is where we are deepest — fuels, lubricants and bulk chemicals, where a load leaving a terminal has to become a bill of lading, a certificate of analysis, a freight cost and eventually an invoice at the right price. We also work across industrial, plant hire, logistics and the charity sector.

We have offices in London and Houston, and clients on both sides of the Atlantic.

How we work

  • Small increments, not big launches. You should feel the benefit in weeks, not at the end of a programme.
  • We learn your vocabulary. You should not have to explain your own business to your integrator.
  • We build things your team can keep alive. Handing back something only we can maintain is a failure, not a retainer.
  • We say when something is a bad idea. Including when it is our idea.

The technology

Salesforce — administration, Tier 2 support, Apex and Lightning development, CPQ and price books, Pardot, GDPR and consent. Microsoft — Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI, Dataverse, Azure Logic Apps. And the interfaces between those and whatever else you run, most often SAP.
