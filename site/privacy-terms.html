Both companies are data controllers for the purposes of UK data protection law. For anything relating to your personal data, write to hello@commerceworks.net and one response will cover both.

What this website collects

Nothing directly. There is no contact form, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. This site sets no cookies, which is why you have not been asked to accept any.

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, which include IP addresses, for security and to keep the site running. We do not use those logs to identify or profile anyone.

When you contact us

If you email or telephone us, we hold whatever you send: your name, your contact details, the organisation you work for and what you tell us about your systems. We use it to answer you and, if it goes further, to prepare a proposal. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: you have approached us about work, and replying is what you would expect. Once you become a client, the basis becomes performance of a contract.

We do not add enquirers to a marketing list, and we do not sell or share contact details with anyone.

Client data in the systems we build

Most of the personal data we come near belongs to our clients, not to us: their staff, their customers, their contacts, held in the systems we build and support. In that work we act as a processor, on written instructions from the client, under the terms of the agreement with them. We do not use client data for our own purposes.

Who else sees your data

  • Our hosting provider, which serves this website and holds the server logs described above.
  • Our email provider, which carries and stores correspondence.
  • Our accountants and professional advisers, where a matter requires it.
  • Anyone we are legally required to tell, such as a regulator or a court.

Some of these providers operate outside the United Kingdom, including in the United States. Where personal data is transferred abroad, it is protected by the safeguards UK law requires, such as the UK addendum to the standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become work are kept for up to 24 months, so we can pick up the thread if you come back to us, and are then deleted. Records relating to clients are kept for seven years after the end of the relationship, which is the period our tax and accounting obligations require.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, ask us to restrict what we do with it, and object to our processing it on the basis of legitimate interests. Write to hello@commerceworks.net. We will respond within one month.

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled a request, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk, or by telephone on 0303 123 1113. We would rather you came to us first, but it is your right either way.
